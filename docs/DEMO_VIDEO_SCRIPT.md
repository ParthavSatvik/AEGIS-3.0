### Scene 1 (0:00–0:18)
A campus water pump fails. The backup pump runs on a battery whose health reading is forty-seven minutes old. The fastest recovery plan might quietly drain the reserve that critical loads depend on. This is AEGIS, the Counterfactual Safety Agent. It tests every plan before you commit.

### Scene 2 (0:18–0:40)
Three plans. Plan A is fastest, twelve minutes, but it breaches the thirty percent reserve in five of nine hypothetical battery conditions. Plan B takes twenty-five minutes and holds almost everywhere. Plan C protects the reserve, but takes a hundred and eighty minutes against a sixty-minute objective. Reserve protection versus the deadline, made visible.

### Scene 3 (0:40–1:08)
Now the live incident. AEGIS flags the stale reading. Even if an operator, or an LLM, pushes Plan A, the independent, deterministic safety gate blocks it. Rule R1, the reserve floor. AEGIS finds a concrete counterexample: under a degraded battery, Plan A ends at twenty-one percent, below thirty. And it runs a diagnostic only because the diagnostic is worth its cost.

### Scene 4 (1:08–1:38)
Plan B passes the gate, but nothing runs yet. A human must approve, and the gate re-checks on fresh telemetry first. Approved. Service is restored in twenty-nine simulated minutes, and verified. Then a second fault: the grid trips. AEGIS replans to a load-shedding plan, and asks for approval again.

### Scene 5 (1:38–2:03)
The Shadow Twin runs one plan across nine hypothetical conditions: battery health against extra drain. Green holds the reserve, red breaches it. These are what-if simulations. They are not telemetry, and not a safety guarantee.

### Scene 6 (2:03–2:25)
Here is real national demand data from the National Power Portal, measured, in megawatts, and used only as context. Only its normalized shape drives a clearly labelled simulated stress replay. National megawatts never touch the battery calculations.

### Scene 7 (2:25–2:50)
On our synthetic benchmark, AEGIS has zero hard-limit violations where the baselines have over a hundred, at the cost of more simulated downtime. And one of our own pre-registered hypotheses is reported as not supported. Every decision exports as an auditable proof record.

### Scene 8 (2:50–3:00)
AEGIS. Decision support that finds the failure before you commit to the fix. Simulation only. Independent validation is the next step.
